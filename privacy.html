Privacy Policy

TCG Scan Card · Last updated: November 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information TCG Scan Card ("the app", "we") collects, how we use it and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Card photos: when you scan or upload a card, the photo is sent to our servers and the service that runs our AI analysis, so we can identify the card and, if you ask for it, estimate its grade.

Collection data: the collections and decks you create, and your scan and grading history, so we can show them and their value. Your price watchlist is stored only on your device.

Account data: the app creates an anonymous account for you, with no name or email. Its identifier and the app version are used to sign you in and to apply the daily limits. It is kept in your device's secure storage, so it can survive reinstalling the app.

Purchase data: the status of your subscription and its transaction identifier, so we can unlock PRO features.

Feedback: the message, rating and optional email you choose to send us.

Who processes your data

We use service providers to run the app: Supabase hosts accounts and collection data, and the server that provides our card catalog and AI analysis receives your card photos, which may be processed by third-party AI providers. They process this data only to provide the service.

How we use card photos

Photos are used only to identify cards and produce grading estimates. We do not sell your photos and we do not use them for advertising. Photos may be kept for a limited time to improve recognition quality and are then deleted.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We can see the status of your subscription, but we never see or store your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we tell you when a scan or grading finishes while you're away from the app. You can turn them off anytime in iOS Settings.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and its data at any time in Settings, under Delete Account. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription, which you manage in your Apple ID settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
